Measuring Tips

Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape measure horizontal.

Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose.

Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.

Length: For tops, measure from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem.

Shoulder: Measure from one shoulder point to the other across the back.

Sleeve: Measure from the shoulder seam to the end of the cuff.

Inseam: Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg.

Note: All measurements are in inches. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit.
